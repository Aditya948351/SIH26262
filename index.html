Mathematical Non-Repudiation Architecture
Algorithm: H(i) = SHA256( H(i-1) || Actor || Action || Payload )

Every regulatory docket transition, deficiency issuance, clock custody switch, and document verification is permanently bound to the preceding state by a SHA-256 rolling hash. Any clandestine mutation or illicit backdating breaks the downstream chain instantly with an O(N) linear mismatch.
